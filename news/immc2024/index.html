IMMC 2024 Presentations

Published on Sept 29, 2024

Members of MIStraLab research group participated in the 22nd International Metallurgy and Materials Congress (IMMC 2024), held in Istanbul between September 19–21, 2024. The event served as a major forum bringing together leading academics and industry professionals from across the materials science sector. Our team actively contributed to the scientific program across multiple formats:

  • Oral Presentations: Gökhan Tarman and Umut Can Gülletutan delivered talks highlighting our latest research findings.
  • Poster Sessions: Uğur Akbulut and Şamil Dinçer presented their work, engaging with attendees during the technical sessions.
