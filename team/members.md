# Team Data

Single source for Team list and member detail pages.

Entry type:
- `## member:slug`

Rules:
- `group` is ordered by dominance and comma-separated.
- Example: `group: phd, msc_alumni`
- `page` is `yes` or `no`.
- `alumni_year` is required only when at least one alumni group exists.
- If `msc_thesis` exists with `group: ... msc_alumni`, person appears in M.Sc. alumni table.
- If `phd_thesis` exists with `group: ... phd_alumni`, person appears in Ph.D. alumni table.
- Multi-value fields can use either ` | ` or bullet-list style:
  `field:`
  `- item 1`
  `- item 2`
