About Us

Materials Informatics Strategies Laboratory (MIStraLab) at Middle East Technical University is a multidisciplinary research group dedicated to advancing materials science through data-driven methods and machine learning. At MIStraLab, we tackle complex challenges in materials discovery, design, and optimization by integrating computational modeling, informatics, and experimental data. Our mission is to accelerate scientific breakthroughs by developing tools and methodologies at the intersection of materials science and artificial intelligence.

Driven by curiosity and collaboration, our team works on projects spanning academic research and real-world industrial applications, with the goal of shaping the future of smart materials and sustainable technologies.
